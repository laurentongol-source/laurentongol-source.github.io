How do you use the quiz?

Basics of the Quiz Structure

  • To start the quiz, go to the previous screen and click the start button!
  • The quiz will throw you straight into it ! Go through each of the questions, clicking your answer.
  • You will not be able change your option after your answer.
  • ⓘ Ensure to not leave during the quiz as all of your progress of the quiz will be lost!

Your progress

  • Your progress will be saved after each of your attempts.
  • They will be kept in the results page!
