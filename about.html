ABOUT PIECEFINDER

_____________

Piecefinder is an independent project created by a single person. (ME!) This website is a tool designed to help writers, artists, and creators to build one of the most important aspects of their work: characters. Creating a character is a complex and difficult process, but with the right guidance, it can be one of the most fun and rewarding parts of the creation of the story. Piecefinder aims to provide that guidance and help you create a character that is unique, interesting, and memorable. This website includes a quiz that will help determine your learning progress along with explainations of the different aspects of character creation.

Happy writing!
